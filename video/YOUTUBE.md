**Description:**

Hearthnote is a private notebook for the whole care circle around someone who is looked after at
home. Anyone writes what happened; NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super on Nebius Token Factory files each note
into observations, medication changes, tasks and routines in about a second and a half, and
Nemotron 3 Ultra writes a weekly family brief that lines up dates across people and turns concerns
into questions for the GP. Names, addresses and phone numbers are withheld before any text leaves
the family's server, emergencies are flagged offline before any model is involved, and every line
of every brief links to the note it came from.

Built for the Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon (Personal AI track) by Rui Codex, Belgium.
