**Description:**

Hearthnote is a private notebook for the whole care circle around someone who is looked after
at home. Anyone writes what happened; NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super on Nebius Token Factory files
each note into observations, medication changes, to-dos and routines, and Nemotron 3 Ultra
writes a weekly family brief that lines up dates across people and turns concerns into
questions for the GP. Names, addresses and phone numbers are withheld before any text leaves
the family's server, and emergencies are flagged offline before any model is involved.

Built for the Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon (Personal AI track) by Rui Codex, Belgium.

Live demo (no login, fictional household): https://hearthnote.ruicodex.com
Code (MIT): https://github.com/RuiCodex-dev/hearthnote
