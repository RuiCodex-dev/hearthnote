# Fonts

Both families are self-hosted (latin subset, variable woff2 from Google Fonts) and licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).

- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next - Braille Institute of America. Chosen because it was designed
  for readers with low vision, which matters in an app read by older relatives and tired
  carers at the end of a day.
- Newsreader - Production Type. Used for headings and the weekly brief.
